**为什么不用固定的 depth**

如果按跳数限制，遇到高频实体时，第二跳就可能扩出成千上万条记忆，开销失控。按节点预算加单实体扇出上限来控制，成本更可预测。想让它走得更深，就把 `budget` 调高，或者适当放宽单实体的扩展上限。

# 🪞Notes


对，有控制“走多远”的参数，但 Hindsight **没有直接让你设“跳几步”的 depth / hops 参数**。它控制的是**最多探索多少个节点**，也就是按节点预算来限制，不是按层数。

**1.** **`budget`****：最主要的旋钮（每次 recall 时传）**

可选 `low` / `mid` / `high`，默认 `mid`。它会被换算成一个整数 `recall_budget`，四路检索都会用到。⁠[![](https://img.notionusercontent.com/ext/https%3A%2F%2Fwww.google.com%2Fs2%2Ffavicons%3Fdomain%3Dhindsight.vectorize.io%26sz%3D64/raw?imgBuildSrc=createUnsignedImageVariantURL)](https://hindsight.vectorize.io/0.6/developer/configuration)⁠ 落到图检索上，就是**最多探索** **`recall_budget`** **个节点**。⁠[![](https://img.notionusercontent.com/ext/https%3A%2F%2Fwww.google.com%2Fs2%2Ffavicons%3Fdomain%3Dhindsight.vectorize.io%26sz%3D64/raw?imgBuildSrc=createUnsignedImageVariantURL)](https://hindsight.vectorize.io/1.0/developer/retrieval)⁠

|budget|适合|代价|
|---|---|---|
|`low`|简单查询|快，但可能漏掉间接关联|
|`mid`|大多数查询|覆盖和速度比较平衡|
|`high`|需要多跳的复杂查询|更全，但更慢|

官方举的例子是：“Alice 的经理的团队做了什么？”这种需要 Alice → 经理 → 团队多跳的问题，适合用 `high`。⁠[![](https://img.notionusercontent.com/ext/https%3A%2F%2Fwww.google.com%2Fs2%2Ffavicons%3Fdomain%3Dhindsight.vectorize.io%26sz%3D64/raw?imgBuildSrc=createUnsignedImageVariantURL)](https://hindsight.vectorize.io/1.0/developer/retrieval)⁠ 文档也把 budget 描述为同时影响图遍历深度、候选池大小和重排序的参数。⁠[![](https://img.notionusercontent.com/ext/https%3A%2F%2Fwww.google.com%2Fs2%2Ffavicons%3Fdomain%3Dhindsight.vectorize.io%26sz%3D64/raw?imgBuildSrc=createUnsignedImageVariantURL)](https://hindsight.vectorize.io/0.7/developer/retrieval)⁠

**2. 图检索相关的环境变量（自托管时配置）**

|变量|作用|默认值|
|---|---|---|
|`HINDSIGHT_API_GRAPH_RETRIEVER`|图检索算法|`link_expansion`|
|`HINDSIGHT_API_LINK_EXPANSION_PER_ENTITY_LIMIT`|每个实体最多扩展出多少条记忆，防止“用户”这种高频实体一下扩出一大片|`200`|
|`HINDSIGHT_API_LINK_EXPANSION_TIMEOUT`|图扩展查询的超时时间（秒）|`10`|

超时后，world / experience 类型会退回只用语义链接和因果链接扩展，observation 类型则不返回图结果。⁠[![](https://img.notionusercontent.com/ext/https%3A%2F%2Fwww.google.com%2Fs2%2Ffavicons%3Fdomain%3Dhindsight.vectorize.io%26sz%3D64/raw?imgBuildSrc=createUnsignedImageVariantURL)](https://hindsight.vectorize.io/developer/configuration)⁠

**3. 默认算法** **`link_expansion`** **是怎么走的**

- **起点**：先用语义检索找到一批“种子”记忆，再从它们扩展出去。
- **沿哪些边扩展**：实体共现、语义近邻（kNN）、因果链接。
- **目标延迟**：100ms 以内。

⁠[![](https://img.notionusercontent.com/ext/https%3A%2F%2Fwww.google.com%2Fs2%2Ffavicons%3Fdomain%3Dhindsight.vectorize.io%26sz%3D64/raw?imgBuildSrc=createUnsignedImageVariantURL)](https://hindsight.vectorize.io/0.6/developer/configuration)⁠

每个候选的得分由几个信号相加，其中实体重合的得分是 `tanh(共享实体数 × 0.5)`。共享的实体越多分越高，但会逐渐封顶。⁠[![](https://img.notionusercontent.com/ext/https%3A%2F%2Fwww.google.com%2Fs2%2Ffavicons%3Fdomain%3Dhindsight.vectorize.io%26sz%3D64/raw?imgBuildSrc=createUnsignedImageVariantURL)](https://hindsight.vectorize.io/0.9/developer/retrieval)⁠
